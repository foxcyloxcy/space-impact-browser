# Historical and Technical References

Research as of 2026-10-31. External links are references, not assets authorized for reuse.

## Primary / high-confidence
- Nokia 2220/2260 user guide, Space Impact instructions (manual reproduction): https://www.manualslib.com/manual/920733/Nokia-22200.html?page=80 . Confirms controls: 8/0 vertical, */# horizontal, 1/3 normal fire, 4/6 bonus fire.
- Nokia NKW-1 user guide, games section: https://www.manualslib.com/manual/3979525/Nokia-Nkw-1.html?page=100 . Confirms the game objective, progression and game settings.

- Historical photo and overview: https://www.sify.com/gaming/the-impact-of-space-impact/ . Useful for original phone-screen references.
- Photo gallery of original LCD game: https://photogallery.indiatimes.com/gadgets/news-events/nokia-3310-the-wonder-years/4-jpg/articleshow/57533626.cms . Useful for HUD and pixel density.
